## App details

**App name:** Periodic Table Poker

**Short description** (max 80 characters):
```
Texas Hold'em with 118 element cards. Learn science through play.
```

**Full description** (max 4000 characters):
```
Periodic Table Poker is an educational card game that combines Texas Hold'em with the 118 elements of the periodic table.

PLAY THE GAME
• Deal hole cards, flop, turn, and river—just like poker
• Build your best 5-card hand from element cards
• Hand value = total atomic mass of your 5 cards
• Special molecule combos (H₂O, NaCl, CHONP) trigger bonus highlights

LEARN SCIENCE
• Each card shows an element's symbol, number, name, and atomic mass
• The best hands feature elements essential to life (DNA: C, H, O, N, P)
• Submit your best hands to the community hierarchy on GitHub
• Vote and propose rankings—the community builds the taxonomy together

PERFECT FOR
• Students learning chemistry
• Teachers and classrooms
• Science enthusiasts
• Anyone who loves poker and wants to think about elements

Play locally, share globally. Made by Nathan Gamal Nasser.
```
